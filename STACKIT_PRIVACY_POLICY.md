# Privacy Policy for Stackit

**Last updated:** February 11, 2026

## Introduction

Stackit ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Stackit mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

## Information We Collect

### Camera Permission
Our App requires camera access to scan business cards. The camera is only activated when you choose to scan a card. We do not access your camera without your explicit permission, and we do not record or store photos beyond the immediate scanning process.

### Contact Information
When you scan business cards or manually add contacts, the information you save (names, phone numbers, email addresses, company details, notes) is stored:
- Locally on your device
- On secure cloud servers (Supabase) if you enable cloud sync

### Calendar Data
If you create events, reminders, or tasks linked to your contacts, this information is stored locally on your device and synced to our secure servers if cloud sync is enabled.

### Account Information
If you create an account for cloud sync features, we collect:
- Email address (for authentication)
- User ID (automatically generated)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Scan and recognize text from business cards
- Store and manage your contact information
- Sync your data across your devices (if enabled)
- Provide calendar and reminder functionality
- Deliver the core features of the App

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
By default, all your data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms.

### Cloud Sync (Optional)
If you enable cloud sync:
- Your data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
- Data is stored on Supabase servers with industry-standard security
- You can disable cloud sync at any time

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

**Supabase** - For cloud data storage and synchronization
- Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

**RevenueCat** - For in-app purchase management
- Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

**Google ML Kit** - For text recognition from business cards (processing happens on-device)
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with anyone except:
- Service providers necessary to operate the App (Supabase, RevenueCat)
- When required by law or legal process

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Disable cloud sync
- Export your data
- Revoke camera permissions at any time through your device settings

## Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

## Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as you use the App. If you delete your account, we will delete all your personal data within 30 days.
